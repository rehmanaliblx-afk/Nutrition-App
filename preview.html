Barbell Bench Press

Barbell Bench Press

INTERMEDIATE
Chest
🏋️ Barbell
🎯 Target Muscles
PRIMARY
Pectoralis MajorAnterior Deltoid
SECONDARY
Triceps BrachiiSerratus Anterior
STABILIZERS
Rotator CuffCore
📋 Technique
1
Set up with eyes directly under the bar; grip slightly wider than shoulder-width with a full thumb wrap.
2
Create a stable arch: retract and depress scapulae, press feet firmly into the floor.
3
Unrack and bring the bar over mid-chest. Take a deep breath and brace your core tight.
4
Lower the bar under control to touch the lower chest, keeping elbows at ~45–75°.
5
Drive the bar back up in a slight arc, exhaling at the top. Lock out without hyperextending.
💡 Coaching Tips
✓Keep wrists stacked directly above elbows throughout the movement.
✓Drive through the legs to create full-body tension and improve bar speed.
✓Think "chest up to the bar" rather than pushing the bar down for better activation.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
✗Flaring elbows 90° — puts excessive stress on the shoulder joint.
✗Bouncing the bar off the chest — reduces tension and risks injury.
✗Lifting hips off the bench to grind a heavy rep.

Vitamin D

☀️
Vitamin D
Cholecalciferol (D3) / Ergocalciferol (D2)
Fat-Soluble Vitamin
🧬 Role & Function

Regulates calcium absorption and bone mineralisation. Critical for immune function, muscle contraction, and mood regulation. Acts as a steroid hormone throughout the body.

⚠️ Deficiency Symptoms
•Bone pain and muscle weakness
•Fatigue and depression
•Frequent infections and impaired immunity

Fatty FishEgg YolksFortified MilkMushroomsCod Liver Oil
